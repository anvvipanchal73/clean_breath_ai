## PM2.5 Annual Guideline
The WHO 2021 guideline for annual mean PM2.5 is **5 µg/m³**.
The interim targets are IT-1 (35 µg/m³), IT-2 (25 µg/m³), IT-3 (15 µg/m³), and IT-4 (10 µg/m³).

## PM2.5 24-Hour Guideline
The 24-hour mean PM2.5 guideline value is **15 µg/m³** (99th percentile).

## PM10 Guidelines
- Annual mean: **15 µg/m³**
- 24-hour mean: **45 µg/m³**

## Health Effects Summary
Short-term PM2.5 exposure (24-h) is associated with:
- Increased all-cause mortality
- Cardiovascular hospital admissions
- Respiratory hospital admissions
- Acute respiratory symptoms in children

Sensitive groups at greatest risk:
- Infants and children under 5
- Elderly (65+)
- People with cardiovascular or respiratory disease
- Pregnant women

## NO2 Guidelines
- Annual mean: **10 µg/m³**
- 24-hour mean: **25 µg/m³**

## Medical Disclaimer
This knowledge chunk is for general public health guidance only.
Symptoms including chest pain, persistent shortness of breath, or wheezing
should prompt immediate medical consultation.
