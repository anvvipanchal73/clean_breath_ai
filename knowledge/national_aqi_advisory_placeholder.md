## India NAQI Categories and Health Implications

| AQI Range | Category | General Population | Sensitive Groups |
|-----------|----------|--------------------|-----------------|
| 0–50 | Good | No health implications | No precautions needed |
| 51–100 | Satisfactory | Minor breathing discomfort for sensitive | Moderate exercise caution |
| 101–200 | Moderate | Discomfort for people with heart/lung conditions | Avoid prolonged outdoor exertion |
| 201–300 | Poor | Breathing discomfort for most people | Avoid outdoor activity |
| 301–400 | Very Poor | Serious respiratory effects likely | Stay indoors |
| 401–500 | Severe | Health impacts for healthy; serious risk for sensitive | Do not go outdoors |

> NOTE: India NAQI uses different breakpoints from US EPA AQI.
> CleanBreath AI uses US EPA scale (returned by Open-Meteo) but references
> India NAQI categories for user communication.

## Sensitive Groups in India NAQI
- Children
- Elderly
- People with respiratory illness (asthma, COPD)
- People with cardiovascular disease
- Pregnant women

## Medical Disclaimer
If you experience persistent cough, wheezing, chest tightness, or difficulty
breathing, please consult a registered medical practitioner immediately.
This app does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.
