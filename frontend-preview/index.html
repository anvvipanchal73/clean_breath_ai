🔬 Scientific Myth-Busters

Myth: Houseplants clean smog
Fact: Cummings & Waring (2020) proved a typical potted plant cleans ~0.023 m³/h. You need ~8,700 plants to match one standard HEPA purifier.
Myth: Any surgical or cloth mask protects you
Fact: Cloth and surgical masks lack tight facial seals. Over 50-80% of fine PM2.5 bypasses around the sides (Lindsley et al. 2021, JAMA).
